## Format

A data frame with 28915 rows and 40 variables:

- SOURCE:

- NACCID:

  Participant ID

- NACCADC:

  ADC at which subject was seen (100 -9999)

- STUDYDATE:

- SERIESTIME:

- STUDYVISITCODE:

- SERIESNUMBER:

- SERIESTYPE:

- STUDYQC:

- STUDYQCCOMMENT:
